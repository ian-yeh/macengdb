title: Why MacEngDB?
---

Hi, friend. Welcome! 

MacEngDB is dedicated to providing a comprehensive database of engineering companies
and design teams, making it easier for students and professionals to discover
opportunities and connect with the engineering community.

Platforms like **Glassdoor** succeeded because they were built on a foundation of
transparency. When you got on the site, the information you shared wasn't about
*'leaking information'*, but was shared to help job-seekers make better decisions
and prepare for interviews more efficiently. 

That is what exactly **MacEngDB** aims to do. McMaster students sharing how they got
these extremely competitive opportunities, which brings entire ladder up for
everyone else.

Waterloo has an extremely tight community of builders. So does U of T, 
Stanford, Berkeley. They're top schools, for a reason.

There's nothing stopping us. Let's build the community **McMaster** deserves.
